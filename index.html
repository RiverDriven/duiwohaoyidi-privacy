隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

你的数据只属于你 · Your data stays yours
最后更新 · Last updated：2026 年 10 月 31 日 / October 31, 2026

一、数据存在哪里

所有数据都保存在你这部手机的本地存储空间里，包括你写下的心愿、计划、打卡记录、感受与照片。

我们没有服务器，也没有账号体系，因此没有任何一份数据会自动离开你的手机。

二、我们不收集什么

不收集手机号、邮箱、通讯录、位置、相册全库、设备标识符（IDFA/IDFV）中的任何一项。

不接入任何第三方统计、广告或崩溃上报 SDK。

不做跨 App 追踪，不读取剪贴板，不在后台上传。

三、我们记录了哪些本地信息

为便于你了解 App 的行为，App 会在本机记录一份操作日志：你打开了哪个页面、什么时候打了一次卡。它只写在本机，你可以在「设置 > 埋点记录」里逐条查看，也可以一键清空。

如果 App 发生过闪退，崩溃信息会存成本地日志文件，只有你主动导出时才会被带走。

四、权限说明

相机：仅在你主动点「拍照」时调用，照片只写入 App 自己的沙箱目录。

相册：通过系统选择器，App 只能拿到你选中的那一张，不会读取整个相册。

通知：按你为每个计划设定的时间在本机提醒，可随时在系统设置或 App 内关闭。

以上权限均为可选，拒绝不影响核心功能使用。

五、你主动导出和分享时会发生什么

「导出备份」「导出反馈包」「计划分享」都需要你亲手点一次，并通过系统分享面板选择去向（AirDrop、文件 App、邮件等）。内容只会去到你选的地方；除非你主动发送给我们，否则我们不会收到任何内容。

分享计划给他人时，对方拿到的是计划内容本身，不含你的账号身份。

六、数据保留与删除

数据一直保存在你的设备上，直到你删除它。

你可以在 App 内删除单个条目，或使用「删除全部数据」一次性清空；卸载 App 会连同本地数据一并删除，且无法恢复。

由于我们不保存副本，删除后我们无法帮你找回。

七、儿童

本 App 不面向 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息（本身不收集任何个人信息）。如果你认为有儿童在无监护人指导下使用，请联系我们，我们会协助处理。

八、你的权利

因为你使用的数据始终在你自己的设备上，访问、更正、删除、导出都可以由你在 App 内直接完成，无需向我们提出申请。若你所在的司法辖区（如欧盟 GDPR）赋予你额外权利，同样可联系我们。

九、政策更新

本政策随版本更新。重大变更会在 App 内或版本说明中提示，并在本页更新「最后更新」日期。

1. Where your data lives

Everything you write — wishes, plans, check-ins, notes, and photos — is stored locally on your device. The app has no server and no account system, so none of your data is automatically transmitted anywhere.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect your phone number, email address, contacts, location, full photo library, or device identifiers (IDFA/IDFV). We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK. We do not track you across apps, read your clipboard, or upload anything in the background.

3. Local records on your device

To help you understand what the app does, it keeps a local activity log (which screen you opened, when you checked in). This log stays on your device; you can read it entry by entry and clear it at any time under Settings. If the app has ever crashed, a crash log is written locally and only leaves your device if you export it yourself.

4. Permissions

Camera: used only when you tap to take a photo; the photo is written to the app's own sandbox.

Photos: the system photo picker is used, so the app receives only the single item you select and never requests access to your entire library.

Notifications: scheduled locally for the times you set per plan, and can be turned off at any time in system settings or in the app.

All permissions are optional; declining them does not affect the core features.

5. When you export or share

Export Backup, Export Feedback, and Plan Sharing each require you to tap once and then choose a destination in the system share sheet (AirDrop, Files, Mail, and so on). The content goes only where you send it; we do not receive anything unless you deliberately send it to us. When you share a plan, the recipient receives the plan content itself, not any account identity.

6. Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual items, or use Delete All Data to clear everything at once. Deleting the app removes all local data permanently. Because we keep no copy, we cannot recover data after deletion.

7. Children

This app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children (it collects no personal information at all). If you believe a child has used the app without guardian supervision, please contact us and we will help.

8. Your rights

Because your data stays on your own device, you can access, correct, delete, and export it directly in the app without contacting us. If your jurisdiction grants you additional rights (for example, under the EU GDPR), you may also contact us.

9. Changes

This policy is updated together with the app. Material changes will be noted in the app or in the release notes, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
